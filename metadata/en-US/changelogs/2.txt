### Added

- Quick Settings tile (Android 7+) for manual Sleep Mode override.
- Persistent active notification with a "Stop now" action.
- Notification channel support (Android 8+) for reliable delivery.

### Changed

- Sleep Mode bundle: Now toggles Airplane Mode ON, disables Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth, and forces Doze/Idle mode.
- Sleep Mode exit: Unforces Doze/Idle and restores pre-sleep connection states (regardless of manual changes made during sleep).
