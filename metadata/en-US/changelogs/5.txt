Changes in Release 2.2:

 - Fixing the Quick Settings tile show a right‑side arrow (chevron).
 - Resolved an issue with enabling/disabling Auto Sleep mode manually when using the Quick Settings tile in different conditions.

Changes in Release 2.1:

The "Turn on Wi-Fi Automatically" feature was officially introduced in Android 9 (Pie). It added the "wifi_wakeup_enabled" setting, which allows Wi-Fi chips to wake up and generate device wake interrupts even when Wi-Fi is turned off. This app release includes enhancements that ensure the Wi-Fi chip remains completely dormant, which helped reduce battery drain on the tested device by a factor of six (from 12% per 9 night hours to 2%).
